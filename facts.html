Major Discoveries

Home Facts Discoveries Quiz
  • Isaac Newton (1687): Law of Universal Gravitation – Every mass attracts every other mass in the universe.
  • Charles Darwin (1859): Evolution by Natural Selection – Traits that help organisms survive become common in populations.
  • Louis Pasteur & Robert Koch (1860s–1880s): Germ Theory of Disease – Invisible microorganisms cause disease; sterilization prevents illness.
  • Max Planck, Niels Bohr, Werner Heisenberg (Early 1900s): Quantum Mechanics – Atomic-level rules differ; energy comes in discrete quanta.
  • Nicolaus Copernicus (1543): Heliocentrism – Earth and planets revolve around the Sun.
  • Dmitri Mendeleev (1869): Periodic Table – Elements arranged by atomic weight show repeating chemical properties.
  • Marie & Pierre Curie (1898): Radioactivity – Some elements emit energy; atoms can transform into energy.
  • James Watson, Francis Crick & Rosalind Franklin (1953): DNA Double Helix – The blueprint of life stored in chemical sequences (A,T,C,G).
  • Michael Faraday & James Clerk Maxwell (1800s): Electromagnetism – Electricity, magnetism, and light are manifestations of the same field.
  • Alfred Wegener (1912): Continental Drift – Continents were once a single landmass (Pangaea) and drift apart over time.
  • Georges Lemaître (1927): Big Bang Theory – Universe started from a single dense point and has been expanding ever since.
